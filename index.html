BlogFort App SEO writing studio

Write SEO articles in one place.

An SEO writing studio that runs in your browser. Write, proofread, translate and export articles with the SEO score in view, and call the AI whenever you want, with your own Anthropic key.

  • SEO editor with live feedback. Headings, keyword, URL slug, a meta description of up to 155 characters and a 0 to 100 score as you type.
  • AI assistant. Write, improve, optimize for SEO, make the tone more natural, and draft titles, meta tags and conclusions.
  • Natural-tone indicator for Spanish text. Called “Humanizer” inside the app, it flags the stock phrases AI text tends to repeat. It is a style guide, not a guarantee against any detector.
  • Proofreader and translator. Check spelling, grammar and tone, and translate with 6 styles, regional variants and voice dictation.
  • Ready-to-use exports. PDF in your brand color with numbered pages, HTML for WordPress and clean printing. Word (.docx) is on Business.
  • Your articles stay with you. Autosave, an article library and a one-click backup that never includes your API key.

MATRIX Agency dashboard

Run your agency from one private dashboard.

A control panel for the person who runs the business: leads, clients, contracts, money and documents in one place, behind your own password and PIN. Everything stays on your device.

  • Lead pipeline. Follow each prospect through four phases, from detection to the final report.
  • Clients and contracts. Track each client's progress and each contract's margin, and copy a client profile to MWBFA in one click.
  • Finance calculators. Price, discount, tax and cost give you the net margin; the strategic view projects your income over the period.
  • Two vault chambers. Alpha, behind a PIN, holds legal and corporate documents and your internal accounting. Beta holds client contracts. Both warn you before documents expire.
  • Alert traffic light. Red for anything due within 7 days, yellow within 30.
  • Sealed reports. PDF or Word reports in 22 languages, with an authenticity seal anyone can check at blogfort.agency/verify. Ask in your own words, such as “accounting for March”: the AI only interprets the request, and the figures come from your saved data.
  • Safe by default. Five failed attempts lock a credential for 15 minutes. One-click backup to a file and an audit log of what happened.

One MATRIX plan includes everything, in all 22 languages.

Good to know
  • The protection is local: it guards access from this browser and device and does not replace your computer's own security.
  • Accounting figures are estimates based on percentages you set. They are not tax advice: confirm them with your accountant.
  • MATRIX organizes your leads; it does not find them. You can import them from your own search system as a JSON file.

MWBFA Website builder

Build websites for your clients.

A website builder for agencies and freelancers. Pick a template for the client's sector, edit the text right on the page, check the result and export it: one HTML file on Starter, or a ZIP ready to upload on Business. The AI writes the texts block by block, or the whole site from a client profile on Business.

  • 12 templates by sector. Corporate, shop, restaurant, clinic & health, law firm, real estate, hotel & tourism and more, each with color variants.
  • Edit on the page. Click any text to change it; reorder, hide or add sections; preview on desktop, tablet and phone.
  • Design and SEO. Colors, fonts, page title, description and social-sharing details.
  • AI writer. Fill in the client's profile and the AI writes the site's texts in the language you choose. It is instructed to use only what you give it and not to invent figures, awards or reviews. Always read the result and check it before you publish.
  • Checker on your device. It scores SEO, accessibility, privacy, contrast and the 390 px phone view, lists what to fix and applies the corrections you tick, such as the page title, the description and missing image descriptions.
  • Export. One HTML file on Starter; HTML, a ZIP ready to upload and PDF on Business.
  • Business also builds installable PWA apps (digital menu, bookings, catalog, services, loyalty card) and writes voice-assistant scripts for you to paste into your own platform.
