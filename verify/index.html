VERIFY AUTHENTICITY

BlogFort Agency LLC · Check whether a document or an email signed by BlogFort is genuine. The check runs in your browser; nothing is sent anywhere.

What this proves and what it does not: it confirms that the signature was created with a license issued by BlogFort (or directly by BlogFort, in the case of emails) and that the signed content has not been altered. It does not stop someone from editing a PDF in another program, so always compare the printed fingerprint with the one shown on this page.
